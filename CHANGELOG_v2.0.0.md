# Kameen v2.0.0 — the retention update

## A1 Daily directives (mastery curve)
- Every shift opens with a bulletin of standing rules (1 rule early, up to 3 by shift 6): expired papers → citation/warning never advice; 3+ passengers → must be stopped and questioned; plates starting with today's letter → radio check; commercial vehicles → papers inspected; night → no vehicle passes unstopped. Rules rotate per shift.
- Each closed case is graded against the active rules: +35 XP "directive followed", −40 XP and a banner when broken. A 📋 chip in the HUD re-opens the bulletin any time.

## B1 Aftermath radio (delayed variable reward)
- 40–90 s after a decision, dispatch reports the real outcome: a wrongly waved serious case comes back as a collision or stolen-vehicle report (−80 XP, trust −3, red flash); a correct hold comes back as a warrant/prosecutor thanks (+90 XP, confetti). Neutral "arrived safely" chatter sometimes.

## C1 Perks tree (identity & replay)
- Each new rank = 1 skill point. Three branches, six perks with real effects: Investigator — Eagle Eye (first discrepancy flagged), Iron Memory (combo lasts 85 s); Negotiator — Smooth Talker (+15 driver patience), Local Hero (regulars warm twice as fast); Field — Fast Radio (verification 40% faster), Mechanic (wider repair zone). Menu card + modal.

## D2 Rival officer (competition without a server)
- Lt. Adel at the next checkpoint sets a weekly score derived from your own best; menu card shows who leads; he taunts on the radio at shift start and concedes or gloats after your report.

## F1 Cold open (first-minute hook)
- A brand-new career's first shift starts with a wanted vehicle already rolling in under siren: one dramatic decision before the tutorial proper.

## B2 Letters
- End-of-shift mail card: complaints (unsound harsh calls) and commendations (clean serious catches, happy regulars). Three accumulated complaints → the supervisor has a word, −100 XP.

## C3 Cosmetics
- Titles (Eagle of the Checkpoint / Hawk Eye / Legend of the Road) unlocked by commendations, shown as a nameplate above your officer in game; badge colours (gold, silver, blue, red) tint the HUD rank chip.

## G1 Micro-animations
- Paper handed through the window when documents are produced; barrier bounces when it finishes opening/closing; booth light flickers on at dusk; partner nods (checks his notes) whenever you close a case.

## G2 Ducking / G3 Haptics
- Music dips under every dispatch banner with a static tail when it returns. Vibration on stamps, serious catches, aftermath reports, the cold-open siren (mobile).
