# Kameen v2.5.2
- **Shift ends when the timer ends** (3-second grace for the closing banner). Cars still stopped are handed over to the next shift; the game no longer waits up to 150 s for open cases.
- **Promotion celebration**: when a shift promotes you, a full-screen AAA-style level-up plays on the report — rotating gold rays, the new rank badge popping in, "PROMOTED!", the rank name, "+1 skill point", a burst of sparks, fanfare + music sting and a haptic pattern. Tap to dismiss (auto-closes after 6.5 s).
- **Saving every 5 seconds**: the in-game autosave now runs every 5 s, plus a 5-second heartbeat that also saves on the menu, report and panels, plus the existing saves on pause, leaving to the menu, tab hide and page unload — the last progress is never more than 5 s old.
