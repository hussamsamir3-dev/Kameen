# Kameen v2.1.2 — new vehicle wheels fixed
- The nine new vehicles now follow the original wheel rule exactly: the tyre arches are cut out of each body, and the rotating wheel (drawn behind the body, through the arch) is the tyre's own pixels on a strip under the sheet. Tyre circles were located with a Hough transform and both axles locked to the same radius, so wheels sit exactly in the arches and rotate with distance travelled — no more painted static wheels in front of the rotating ones.
- Version 2.1.2.
