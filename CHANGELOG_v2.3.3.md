# Kameen v2.3.3 — pedestrians keep walking
- Pedestrians never stand still: they enter from outside the visible world, walk the far corniche pavement (behind the police car, sign, barrier and booth — never in front of them), and leave the world before despawning.
- Instead of pausing they slow down briefly behind someone walking the same way, drift a little deeper into the pavement to pass someone coming the other way, and slow to a stroll (still moving) while a siren or wanted car is on the road.
- Removed the random stops and the "stand and look" behaviour; the 8-frame stride stays locked to distance.
