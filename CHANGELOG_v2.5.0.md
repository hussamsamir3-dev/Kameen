# Kameen v2.5.0 — adaptive music
- Your four tracks are mixed live as layers, AAA-style: **bed_day** (full in daylight), **bed_night** (fades in as night falls — smooth cross-fade driven by the actual light level), **tension** (rises under a wanted car on the road, rush hour, supervisor inspection, emergencies, the final sprint and an active bet; falls back after), and **sting** (a short flourish on serious catches, warrant arrests, bet wins, a closed Black File and shift end; the beds dip under it, max once per 6 s).
- Layers follow the music/master sliders and mute, duck to 50% under radio calls, pause when the tab is hidden, and start on the first user gesture exactly like the old player. The menu plays the beds slightly quieter.
- The old shuffle playlist is disabled (its files can stay on the server or be deleted).
- New file js/34_music.js; music assets under assets/music/.
