# Kameen v1.9.0 — matched backdrop, interactive repairs, guided dialogue, living world

## Backdrop
- Default backdrop scale zoomed out (41 m) so the painted sidewalk lines up with the game street and props at wide AND zoomed views, on every map. Depth-of-field blur kept.

## Vehicles
- Exhaust rebuilt: smaller, softer, slower puffs with long fades (no more blotchy smoke), gentle idle wisps at the marker.

## Repairs are an interaction
- Jammed barrier / dead generator get a pulsing orange ring and a pointer cursor. Tap → popover: **Fix it myself** (timing-bar mini-game), **Ask the sergeant** (he hurries over and repairs it while you keep working), or **Cancel**.

## Light casting
- Warm ground pools under both floodlights and the booth window, headlight spill on the road for every car in the lane at night (additive, soft radial falloff).

## Dialogue panel — rebuilt for clarity
- 4-step strip at the top (Talk → Papers → Verify → Decide) that tracks where you are; tone selector hidden behind a small "Tone ▾"; only 3 questions shown at a time with "More"; the footer button for the next step glows; driver-notes column hidden on smaller screens; bigger buttons.

## Why players come back (new)
- **Regulars**: six recurring Egyptian drivers (Am Sayed the taxi driver, Hamada the microbus driver, Dr. Mona, Abdo the student, Eng. Nour, Hajj Ramadan) who keep their face and remember you. Fair treatment builds a friendship — friends tip you off about a wanted car behind them; unfair tickets make enemies who complain to the supervisor (trust drops).
- **The Black File**: serious catches drop clues; five clues close a file for +500 XP, confetti and a stamp. Progress pips on the menu.
- **Uniform unlock**: reach rank 6 to switch to the white officer's uniform from the menu (partner takes navy).

## Officers
- Smooth pose transitions driven by the action clock: the stop hand rises through 4 frames and lowers through 4, the pass wave plays its full 8-frame sweep.

## Sound
- All synth voices 20% quieter with longer, rounder decays; music track names no longer appear anywhere.

## Housekeeping
- Version 1.9.0 in every script; new file js/27_depth.js; footer shows the version.
